About Me

A little bit
about myself

I'm Ethan Lazar, a mechanical engineering student at Northeastern University (B.S. expected May 2028) with a minor in aerospace. Originally from Philadelphia, now based in Boston.

I'm currently a Materials Science and Aerospace Engineering Co-op at The Roux Institute in Portland, ME, where I run mechanical testing, prepare samples for microscopy, and co-author research on cold spray and polymer metallization. At AerospaceNU I'm a project lead on the liquid rocket airframe team. I've studied abroad with Northeastern's Global Scholars Program in London and Oakland, and I've made the Dean's List in Fall 2025 and Spring 2026.

Outside of school, you'll find me running, on the track, cooking, or somewhere near an airplane.
