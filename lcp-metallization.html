Characterization & Metallization of a Liquid Crystal Polymer

Characterizing the mechanical properties of liquid crystal polymer (LCP) specimens and their metallic coatings — from Instron testing to optical microscopy to a conference manuscript.

LCP Characterization
Role
Materials Science and Aerospace Engineering Co-op
Organization
The Roux Institute, Northeastern University
Timeline
July 2026 — November 2026
Tools
Instron test frame, MATLAB, optical microscopy, mounting & polishing equipment
Output
Manuscript in preparation for NAMRC

What I did

  • Performed tensile, compression, and three-point bend testing to determine the elastic modulus, strength, and flexural properties of LCP specimens.
  • Developed a method for measuring coating thickness on metallized samples.
  • Mounted and polished composite, polymer, and metal samples for optical microscopy analysis.
  • Co-authored a manuscript in preparation for the North American Manufacturing Research Conference (NAMRC), writing the methods and analysis sections and creating the data figures.
