Tools

Focus
Dual-deploy recovery, bulkhead design, subscale validation

What I'm doing

  • Researched and selected a dual-deploy recovery system based on the weight budget and time constraints.
  • Designed and prototyped bulkheads to anchor the parachutes and house the electronics bay, using SolidWorks for CAD and 3D printing for rapid prototyping.
  • Built and flew a subscale rocket to validate the recovery system and bulkhead design ahead of full-scale integration.
  • Co-lead a team of 10 students, coordinating design reviews, delegating subsystem tasks, and driving the project from concept to test flight.

What I'm learning

Recovery is the part of a rocket where structural, aerodynamic, and pyrotechnic considerations all collide. Leading a 10-person team has been a lesson in scoping a subsystem, coordinating with adjacent teams (avionics, propulsion), and converging on a design that everyone can build against.

Status

Active. The subscale flight is complete, and the team is moving toward full-scale integration. I'll add photos, CAD renders, and test results here as the project progresses.
