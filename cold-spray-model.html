1D Isentropic Model for Particle Velocity in Cold Spray

A MATLAB model that uses 1D isentropic flow relations to predict gas properties along a cold spray nozzle and the velocity at which powder particles hit the substrate.

Cold Spray Model
Role
Model developer
Organization
The Roux Institute, Northeastern University
Timeline
October 2026
Tools
MATLAB
Output
MATLAB program, team presentation, technical report

What I did

  • Reviewed current literature on numerical modeling techniques for cold spray to set the modeling approach and key assumptions.
  • Developed a MATLAB program using 1D isentropic flow relations to compute pressure, temperature, density, and Mach number along the nozzle and predict particle impact velocity.
  • Presented the model's methodology and results to the research team, explaining its assumptions, limitations, and how to use it.
  • Authored a technical report benchmarking the model's accuracy against published models.
